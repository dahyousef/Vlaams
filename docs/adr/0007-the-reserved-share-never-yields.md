# New items are taken out first, and the reserved share never yields to the backlog

A share of every session's places goes to items never met: thirty per cent, and never fewer
than four. It is taken out before due items fill the rest, and it does not shrink however
large the backlog is, for as long as any item remains unmet.

Anyone who knows spaced repetition expects the opposite, reviews first and new material with
what is left, because the well-known tools work that way. This app did too, and nothing
guaranteed that anything was left: from 22 items due the home screen announced no new item,
and from 120 due the session served none, whatever remained to be met. Reviews that wait a
day cost a day. A course that shows nothing new has stopped.

## Consequences

The day's budget is fixed, so a share that never yields leaves the backlog without a ceiling.
With the 439 items of the first level it peaks at about three hundred for a learner who takes
one session a day, and then drains. With the several thousand items of B1 it will not drain.
Before that content lands, either the share has to shrink as the backlog grows or the budget
has to follow it.

Making the share yield is the obvious repair for a growing backlog, and it restores the fault
this project was rebuilt to fix.
