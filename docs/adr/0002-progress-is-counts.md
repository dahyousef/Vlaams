# Progress is reported as counts, and "mastered" is retired

Screens report how many items the learner has met and how many they can produce. No screen
shows a percentage, and the glossary records *mastered* as a term no longer in use.

One unit card previously carried three different percentages with no labels, and the one
displayed largest measured something roughly two weeks of work away per item — so it read
zero through a hundred correct answers. It was exactly accurate and told the learner
nothing. A count moves every day and cannot be both at once.
